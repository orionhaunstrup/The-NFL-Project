Hi there! Welcome to The NFL Project —— my data mining final project.

This project is called "The NFL Project" and its goal is to construct, from scratch,
a full data mining machine that can take in a current NFL player, compare both their
statistics and performace analytics to all past players ever on record, and predict
what the future of their career will look like.

The core question of the whole project was can past historical data be used to
predict the future of current players. To test the validity of my machine, I designed
it to also be able to take past players, take only some early % of their career, and
estimate the remainder of their career (!), comparing the model's predictions to the
actual story of their career path.

After extensive testing, upwards of 70.2% of the time the model gets at least the next
three years very close! I've been surprised and thrilled at how well it performs.

To run it, simply open the file called "The NFL Project.py", and it has nice friendly
intro messages welcoming the user. All will be self explanatory from there.
